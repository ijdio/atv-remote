# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that seem basic.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, [ijdio](https://github.com/ijdio), through GitHub. You can
also use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature. Reports are handled privately.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues that break this code, and
may block people who repeatedly or seriously break it.
